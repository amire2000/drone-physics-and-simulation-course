# Module 2: Physical asset blueprint (URDF)

## By the end, you will be able to

- Describe a quadcopter frame in URDF.
- Derive the frame's principal moments of inertia.
- Test its response to off-axis torque.

## Lessons

1. Define a 650 g, 5-inch quadcopter structure with URDF XML.
2. Calculate `Ixx`, `Iyy`, and `Izz` from a central body and four motor masses.
3. Spawn the model and evaluate asymmetric torque responses.

Prerequisite: [Module 1: Mass and external forces](../01-mass-and-forces/index.md). Next: [Module 3: Propeller aerodynamics](../03-propeller-aerodynamics/index.md).
